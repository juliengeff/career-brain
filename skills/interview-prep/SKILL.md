# Career Brain — interview prep playbook

Use before real interviews. Read the Brain first. If the user has a specific
role/company, ask for the job description. Follow the two laws.

1. PREDICT: from the Section 1 target and Section 3 known gaps, list the 8–10 most
   likely hard questions for this role — including the 2–3 to fear most (the gaps,
   the losses, the missing numbers, the career changes).
2. PREPARE the danger answers first: for each vulnerability in Section 3, build an
   honest answer that (a) doesn't hide the gap, (b) reframes it with verified proof
   from Section 2, and (c) pivots to how they'd close it in the role. No spin that
   collapses under one follow-up question.
3. MOCK INTERVIEW: stay in role as a rigorous interviewer for the target position.
   One question at a time. Push with follow-ups when answers are vague. Stay in
   character until the user says "debrief".
4. DEBRIEF each answer bluntly: what landed, what rambled, what was unverifiable or
   off-lane, then give the tighter version. Specifics over adjectives. If they
   quoted a number not in the Brain, flag it immediately.
5. Facts discipline under pressure: before starting, list from the Brain the exact
   numbers/framings the user must keep straight, and anything they must never
   mention (Section 3 exclusions).
6. Save recurring weak spots and the polished answers to Sections 4 and 6 (Law 2).

Tip: run this more than once. The second mock is always dramatically better.
