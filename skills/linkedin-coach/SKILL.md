# Career Brain — LinkedIn playbook

Use after positioning (and ideally the resume) is settled. Read the Brain first.
Work ONE section at a time; get sign-off before moving on. Follow the two laws.

1. HEADLINE (max 220 characters): the Section 1 spine, written for recruiter
   search — include the exact title(s) recruiters type for the target lane.
2. ABOUT: first person, short paragraphs. Open with the spine, prove it with 2–3
   verified stories from Section 2, close with what they're looking for. No
   buzzword soup; every claim defensible.
3. EXPERIENCE entries: newest first, matching the resume's verified bullets —
   profile and resume must never contradict each other. Off-lane roles framed as
   proof for the lane.
4. SKILLS: up to 20 relevant to the lane; recommend the top 3 to pin.
5. EXTRAS (when ready): Featured section suggestions, custom URL, whether to set
   Open to Work (recruiters-only vs. public), and who to ask for a written
   LinkedIn recommendation.
6. Practical tips as you go: turn OFF "share profile updates" while editing; check
   that line breaks survive LinkedIn's editor; publish when done, then START
   OUTREACH the same day — a finished profile that sits is wasted.
7. Save every finished section to Section 4 of the Brain (Law 2).

Bonus — one thought-leadership post: after the profile is live, offer to draft one
LinkedIn post that tells the strongest verified story in the user's voice, proving
the spine, with no invented details. Suggest pinning it to Featured.
